The philosophy

"The vessel and the food within it are one."

— Kitaoji Rosanjin, ceramicist & chef

We cook under this belief — that every element of a meal, from the dish it rests on to the hand that prepared it, forms a single continuous expression. Influenced by the kitchens of Korea, Japan, and China, shaped by years in the West, this is food that moves between worlds without losing its soul.

The story
behind
the table.

Rosanjin takes its name from Kitaoji Rosanjin — the Japanese polymath who believed that cooking and art were inseparable acts. A ceramicist, calligrapher, and one of the most influential culinary figures of the twentieth century, he embodied the idea that every meal deserves the same care as any work of art.

This pop-up carries that spirit into a contemporary context. Rooted in the flavor traditions of Korea, Japan, and China — fermented, fire-kissed, and precise — each menu is shaped by the season and served in an intimate setting of twelve or fewer guests. Nothing repeats. Every gathering is its own thing.

We are based in Millburn, New Jersey, and host events across the region. Private events, collaborations, and press inquiries are always welcome.
