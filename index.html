The philosophy

— Kitaoji Rosanjin, ceramicist & chef

I'd like my events to pursue this ideal from a different perspective. I want to show the culture and experiences that has influenced my dishes as a single continuous expression.

The story

I want to create events that share my passion and love for food. I believe a dining table is a great vessel for conversation and familiarity.

These events are an expression of my journey as a student studying hospitality and food and beverage management. I want to share my love for everything culinary.

I hope that I get the chance to see you soon!
